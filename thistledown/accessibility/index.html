Accessibility

Effective October 6, 2026

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to find a home or sell a house through our website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Pages use clear headings, labeled form fields, and text descriptions for images.
  • Everything can be reached with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • Layouts work on phones and when text is enlarged, and animation is reduced if your device asks for it.
  • We review the site when we add new features or listings.

Known limitations

Some listing photos may not yet have detailed descriptions. If anything on the site is hard to use, we will get you the information another way, such as by phone or email.
