1. Who we are

This website is operated by [Company legal name, e.g. Thistledown Homes LLC] ("Thistledown Homes," "we," "us"), a real estate investment company that buys houses below market and resells them at more affordable prices. We act as a principal for our own account and are not a licensed real estate brokerage. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and through calls, texts, and email related to it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Property details: the address of a home you ask about, and anything you tell us about your plans or timeline.
  • Buying or investing interests: the homes you ask about, your price range, or your interest in lending or partnering on a project.
  • Communication consent: whether you agreed to receive calls and texts, and when.

Please do not send us Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or similar sensitive information through the website. If a purchase moves forward, that information is handled through the title company or closing attorney.

Information collected automatically

  • Browser storage on your device: described in Cookies and storage below.
  • Standard request data: like every website, our web host and the providers listed below receive your IP address, browser type, and the page requested when your browser loads this site.

We do not use advertising trackers, social media pixels, session recording, or third-party analytics on this site.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry, prepare a cash offer, schedule a walkthrough, or answer questions about a home.
  • To complete a purchase or sale you agree to, working with title companies, inspectors, lenders, and closing attorneys.
  • To send updates you asked for, such as new homes for sale.
  • To keep records required by law, prevent fraud, and protect our rights.

We do not use your information for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the business (for example our web host and form provider, such as Netlify, plus email and phone or texting providers), who may use it only to provide services to us.
  • With parties needed to complete a transaction you agree to, such as a title company, closing attorney, inspector, appraiser, or your lender.
  • If we assign a purchase contract to another buyer, with that buyer, and only after telling you in writing.
  • When required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • With a successor if our business is sold or merged, subject to this policy.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Third-party content

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and sample photographs from Unsplash. When your browser requests those files, those providers receive your IP address and standard request information under their own privacy policies (Google, Unsplash). They do not receive anything you type into our forms.

If we turn on the Trustpilot review widget or a live chat service (such as Tawk.to), those providers load from their own servers and handle any data under their own privacy policies. Messages sent through the chat bubble on this site are stored with our other contact messages.

The map page loads its mapping code from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and map data from OpenFreeMap or another map provider, which receive your IP address and standard request information.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale of personal information and targeted advertising, and we keep optional analytics off.

6. Calls, texts, and email

We call or text you only if you ask us to contact you or check the optional consent box on our form. That consent is not a condition of buying or selling a home. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also ask us by email to stop calling. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link and our mailing address, and we honor opt-outs promptly. We do not call numbers on the National Do Not Call Registry unless you have given us permission or have an existing relationship with us.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including Texas, California, Colorado, and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal information and get a copy of it in a portable format.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete information you provided or that we collected about you.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, and certain profiling. We do not do these things, but you may still submit a request.
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights.

To make a request, email [privacy email address] or call [phone number]. We will verify your request using information you have already given us and respond within 45 days (we may extend once by 45 more days when reasonably necessary, and will tell you if we do). You may use an authorized agent where the law allows. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with the subject line "Privacy appeal," and we will respond within 60 days. If you are not satisfied with the appeal, Texas residents may contact the Texas Attorney General at texasattorneygeneral.gov.

8. Retention and security

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and transaction records for as long as tax, real estate, and other laws require. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.
