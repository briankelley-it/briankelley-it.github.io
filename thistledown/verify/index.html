Trust and safety

Don't take my word for it. Verify it.

Prices below the neighborhood can sound too good to be true, so here is everything you can check yourself, without relying on anything I say.

I will never ask you to wire money to me.

All deposits and closing funds go to a licensed Texas title company. Before sending any money, call the title company yourself using a phone number you looked up independently, never one from an email. If anyone claiming to be me asks for a wire, gift cards, or crypto, it is a scam.

My commitments

How every purchase works.

These protections apply to every home I sell. If anything ever happens differently, walk away and contact me directly.

  1. Closings at a licensed title company

    Your earnest money and closing funds are held in escrow by a licensed Texas title company. You are welcome to choose your own.

  2. See it before you pay anything

    No deposits, holds, or application fees before you have toured the home in person.

  3. Your own inspection and appraisal

    Hire any inspector you like. If you use a mortgage, your lender orders an independent appraisal, which checks the value for you.

  4. Your own agent is welcome

    Bring a licensed real estate agent to represent you. I will work with them.

  5. Full disclosures in writing

    You receive the Texas Seller's Disclosure Notice, plus a lead-based paint disclosure for homes built before 1978.

  6. No pressure, ever

    Take the time you need. A real deal survives a day to think and a call to your own lawyer.
